# Transform your materials into finished products during manufacturing

- To adapt to customer needs, you must decide how and when to transform your purchased ingredients.

## Customize your inventory management to transform products

```
* This choice mainly depends on the customization and lead time expected by the customer.

* At the supermarket
* At the restaurant
* At an artisan's shop 
* 100% custom-made
```

## In summary

```
* The purpose of manufacturing is to add value by transforming raw materials 
  into finished products for the end customer.

* To be able to transform products, 
  the factory must organize its workshops according to the flexibility, 
  volume, and product mix offered to customers.

* Depending on product customization 
  and the lead time expected by the customer, 
  the transformation will be differentiated
```
