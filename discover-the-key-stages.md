# Discover the key stages

## Respond to Constraints Throughout the Supply Chain

- The goal of the Supply Chain is to deliver to the customer on time and ensure the company is profitable.

```
As life is generally not a smooth sailing, 
you will likely need to take into account numerous constraints to meet your customers' expectations.

These constraints may be:

* Related to lack of information (When you have erratic or incomplete customer orders)
* Physical (For example, lack of space in the refrigerator)
* Financial (Because the Supply Chain cannot exist without cash flow)

``` 
- PS : Remember that we saw how to maximize cash flow in the previous chapter ? 

## The key is to accelerate the flow of materials.

- To respond to constraints and accelerate flows, major industrial methodologies propose to:

```
* Reduce lead time;
* Eliminate constraints;
* Reduce losses by executing correctly the first time;
* Synchronize incoming flows with outgoing flows.

PS : And if you manage to do all three at the same time, it's the jackpot for cash flow.
```
