## Generate cash flow through the operational horizon

- The operational horizon is the practice of aligning a company's resources with the real needs of customers to generate cash flow.

```
The company will make short-term decisions (over a few hours or even a few days) on what to...

* buy
* produce
* transfer within its distribution network
* choose which product to fill the truck and deliver to the supermarket

These operational decisions give the order to execute the flow of materials.
```
