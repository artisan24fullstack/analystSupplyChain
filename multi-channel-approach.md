# Position products close to customers in points of sale

## The objective of the point of sale 

### Evolution of commerce: towards a new era

- With the development of e-commerce, 

```
* physical points of sale are no longer the only places 
* where consumers can choose and buy a product.
```

### Adaptation of points of sale

- Therefore, points of sale have had to adapt so that consumers continue to physically visit stores.

### Multichannel commerce: an evolving shopping experience

- In companies offering online sales and points of sale, multi-channel has developed. 

```
* It allows for multiplying purchasing experiences:

* comparing products and brands on the Internet;
* going to a point of sale to physically look at and test the product;
* ordering and buying on the Internet or by phone to optimize the price;
* picking up the product at a point of sale (free pickup in 1 hour);
* returning the product to a point of sale
```

### With the development of multichannel, 

- The store is no longer necessarily the point of sale. 

```
* In recent years, independent businesses have also 
* become pickup points for Internet deliveries, 
* which allows new consumers to enter the store 
* and increases the chances of their opportunistic sales.

* These pickup points help limit delivery costs, 
* as the last mile transport is the most expensive 
* and complicated to manage.
```

## In summary

```
* The objective of the point of sale is to make the product available 
* and close to the customer to encourage purchase.

* The point of sale must have the right assortment of products 
* and the right stock levels to maximize sales.

* With the development of online sales, 
* the store has evolved to become a place to choose products 
* and pick them up physically, 
* but is no longer necessarily the place 
* where the sale is concluded.

* Now that the finished products are in-store
```
