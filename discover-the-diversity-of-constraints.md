
# Discover the diversity of constraints


- Each Supply Chain environment has its own unique constraints that require adaptation. 
- While we share a common goal of customer satisfaction and common concepts like the three flows
- Different Supply Chains must be tailored to their specific constraints.
- These constraints can occur at each stage of the Supply Chain. 

## In the energy industry : 
- Electricity production must be adjusted to demand as storage is not possible. 
- For example, more power needs to be generated in winter for heating.

## The healthcare sector :
- Must manage health safety. 
- Certain medications or vaccines need to be stored and preserved at controlled temperatures.
  
## The food industry :
- Must handle short expiration dates for fresh products. 
- The stock quantity of yogurts should not exceed the use-by date, otherwise products must be discarded.
  
## In metallurgy : 
- Stopping or starting blast furnaces takes weeks, 
- So steel production must be anticipated and smoothed to avoid halting manufacturing.
  
## In retail : 
- Technical products have increasingly shorter lifespans. 
- Stock-outs must be avoided during the launch of the latest smartphone to prevent missed sales, 
- As market share cannot be recovered when the next model is released in a few months.
  
## In fashion : 
- Managing stock for all sizes, 
- Particularly XXS and XXL, is critical to avoid missing sales opportunities 
- Having unsold items at the end of a collection.

### In summary

- The constraints of a Supply Chain are multiple and must be taken into account in its design 
- Subsequently managed with the flow of information to respond to customers and each environment.
