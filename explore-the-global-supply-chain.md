## Let's return to the standard diagram of a supply chain for ...

### A supply chain for intensive outdoor agriculture

- Let's return to the standard diagram of an intensive outdoor agriculture Supply Chain in France.
  
```
To sell tomatoes, farmers borrow from banks to buy seeds, 
treatment products, and agricultural equipment. 
They cultivate their land to harvest during the season.

In front of his farm, the farmer sells directly from his roadside stand.

He then sells through the large-scale distribution network. 
Trucks come to collect the tomatoes every day and deliver them to supermarkets.

Fo#r the food processing industry, the farmer sells tomatoes that will be transformed into tomato juice. 
The tomato juice will then be transported to supermarkets to be sold to the final consumer.
```

### An Automotive Supply Chain

- Below you will find the diagram of an automotive Supply Chain, focusing on the steel body:

```
Steel ore is extracted from large mines;

It is then transported by ship to refining plants with blast furnaces, 
where it is mixed with other metals and melted through coal combustion. 

The large plates resulting from continuous casting are then rolled 
(rolled, crushed) to form rolls with the expected characteristics;

These steel rolls are then transported by train to automobile factories. 
They are cut, bent, stamped, and welded to create the car's body. 

Before entering the assembly line, the body is painted to prevent rust 
and achieve the color requested by the customer.

The finished car is transported by train, ship, or truck to the dealership 
on the corner of your street, where you ordered your vehicle.
```

### An Electrical Supply Chain:

- When you turn on the light in your living room 
- You don't imagine the journey of electrons to your bulb. 
- Let's consider the case of a green electrical Supply Chain, sourced from hydroelectricity. 

```
The dam holds back part of the flowing water and creates a lake. 
This lake constitutes the water stock, thus allowing the storage of renewable energy.

Water is propelled onto a turbine, which, combined with a generator, creates electricity.

This electricity is transported in the primary High Voltage network, the large electrical poles.

Transformers will lower the voltage to reach the secondary network, called Low Voltage.

The secondary network will transport electricity close to your home.

Finally, transformers near your home adapt the voltage for domestic consumption, 
and the last cables will go to your meter.

The meter measures your consumption, 
then your electrical panel will distribute electricity throughout your house
... up to the bulb in your living room.
```

### A Music Supply Chain

- When I listen to my favorite album on my phone with my subscription, 
- I'm caught up in the emotion of the moment, in the rhythm... 
- I don't necessarily have in mind all the stages of this musical Supply Chain!

```
Your favorite artist writes your future hit;

He or she records the song in a studio. Once edited and arranged, here's the finished product;

Several channels will be used for the distribution of the track;

For the die-hard CD fans, the album will be pressed, 
then physically distributed to stores to reach the end consumer;

For electronic music aficionados, the album will be encoded and uploaded to servers. 

To be listened to, the data will pass through optical fibers, 
then telephone operators before being decoded on your phone. 
All this, so that you can listen to good quality sound;

Finally, for the absolute fans, the singer will travel to meet them at a concert.
```
