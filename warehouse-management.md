# Store your finished products in the distribution center

## Store the goods in the warehouse

- A warehouse is the place where products are received 

```
* in uniform batches and in large quantities, 
* briefly stored, and then broken down into small orders 
* of different references requested by customers.
```

## Management of Goods Storage

- Between their entry and exit from the warehouse, goods will be stored.

## Storage Criteria

- The storage method in the warehouse depends 
  
```
* on the nature of the products 
* and their constraints ... 
* as well as the volumes of the products.
```

## Stock Control and Management

```
* a warehouse worker will verify the reliability of stock quantities by taking inventory;
* the manager will ensure that teams are trained in respecting the cold chain.
```

## Optimization by the WMS System

- Flows and stocks in the warehouse are managed, and can be optimized, by the WMS. 
- The WMS is the specialized information system for managing warehouses 
- The equivalent of the TMS which manages transport


### Route goods within the warehouse

```
* To move products between the entrance, 
* storage areas, and exit of the warehouse, 
* warehouse workers use forklifts. 

* These trucks allow them to transport more goods, 
* faster and with less risk of injury
```

### Warehouse Automation
- Handling equipment can be manual, but it is becoming increasingly automated. 
- We will need more technicians to perform maintenance.

## In summary

```
* The main activities of a warehouse are:

* receiving and storing supplier orders;
* storing them;
* preparing and shipping customer orders;
* managing inventory and personnel.

* Now that finished products are made available in warehouses close to the end customer,
```
