#  Visualize the Supply Chain flows

## In what order should flows be considered to manage the Supply Chain ?

- The information flow, the material flow, then the financial flow

* The first flow is the information flow, to take into account the needs of customers.
* The second flow is the material flow, to "do" according to what the customer wants.
* The financial flow begins when the material flow is put in place to pay suppliers and receive payment from customers
  
## To organize these 3 flows

- you will need to implement Supply Chain Management (SCM): 

```
that is, the art of designing, planning, executing, controlling, and measuring flows to create value for the company.
```  

## According to the Association for Supply Chain Management (APICS), 

- SCM is the design, planning, execution, control, and monitoring of supply chain activities
   
```
with the objective of:

* creating net value;
* building a competitive infrastructure;
* leveraging worldwide logistics;
* synchronizing supply with demand;
* measuring performance globally
```

- SCM encompasses the entire process from sourcing raw materials to delivering finished products to end consumers, 
  focusing on optimizing efficiency, minimizing costs, and maximizing customer value

##  macro value stream mapping (macro VSM)

- We can represent the 3 flows with a tool called macro value stream mapping (macro VSM). 
- The VSM allows you to represent the information exchanged from your customers to your suppliers 
- and the material flows from your suppliers to your customers.

```
The information flow is a transfer of useful information from the customer to the supplier. 

The idea is to identify customer expectations: 

Based on customer expectations, you design and plan your Supply Chain:

Where to buy: 
Where and how to manufacture: 
How to distribute: 
```

- PS : This is the first flow to consider, because without customer orders, there is no Supply Chain.


### Supply Chain Execution

- Once the consumer's need has been defined, it's time to execute, which means to act. 
- This involves the flow of materials from the supplier to the customer. 
- Raw materials are procured, manufactured, and then distributed through the distribution network, stores, or hypermarkets.

### Financial Flows

- Finally, the financial flow stems from the material flow: you pay your suppliers, employees, and for your machines, 
- and in return, your customers pay you when they receive their products.

### SCM Flow Management

- SCM should allow you to control and measure these flows to determine 
- if your Supply Chain is meeting customer needs with optimal company profitability.

### Flow Optimization

- Flow has always been the foundation of major industrial management methods to maximize a company's cash flow. 
- I need to reduce the time between purchasing raw materials (I pay my suppliers) 
- and selling finished products (my customers pay me). 
- To achieve this, I must accelerate the material flow.

## In summary

```
- The Supply Chain is the management of incoming and outgoing movements
  of information, materials, and money within a company to deliver to the customer on time.
- The three types of flows are information flows, material flows, and financial flows.
- The objective is to add value to the product (in manufacturing or distribution) to ensure the profitability of the company.
- The tool called macro VSM (Value Stream Mapping) is used to map and analyze these flows.
```
