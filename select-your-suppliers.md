# Start with the flow of materials via suppliers


## What are the first physical movements of your Supply Chain, of your material flows ?

```
Based on your make or buy needs and types of purchases, 
you will request quotes from your various suppliers. 

A multi-criteria negotiation phase will then begin.

The ideal is always to choose suppliers 
who are aligned with your strategy at a given time T 
and will be able to accompany you in your future developments.

You will have a differentiated, segmented treatment of each type of supplier:

* commodity suppliers 
* strategic suppliers

1. The overall acquisition cost, price, payment terms

2. Product quality, lead time and lead time reliability

3. Service and Supply Chain maturity, culture
```

## Manage the supply

- You will need to manage the supply of your products, 
- which means knowing who manages the physical flow to deliver 
- and organize raw material stocks. 
- To do this, several options are available to you.

```
Management by your procurement officers

* Procurement officers follow up, 
* prioritize supplier purchase orders based on stock levels and demand.

Management by suppliers

* In this case, the supplier manages our raw material stocks. 
* During contract negotiation, you will have defined the target stock level 
* and administrative aspects (orders and payment).

Shared supply management (SSM) or Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) 

* The supplier delivers your products to you. 
* They are in stock; however, you delegate inventory management to your supplier. 
* During negotiations with them, you will have previously defined minimum and maximum stock thresholds. 
* This will ensure better availability of your preferred products.

```
- PS : It's convenient for your cash flow. This will obviously have been negotiated beforehand

## Manage the reception

- It's D-day! The delivery driver arrives with your products. 
- You check the quantity and overall condition of the packages 
- Before accepting them and signing the delivery note.
- Before storing in stock, in the large refrigerator, 
- In some cases, a quality control check at reception is necessary.

```
For quality control at reception, 
there are 3 levels of control depending on the criticality 
of the failures encountered and their frequency:

* Systematic control: all deliveries are checked;
* Random control: deliveries are checked at random (according to rules to be defined);
* No control
```
