# Return a product to the sender with Reverse Logistics

## Discover Reverse Logistics  

- Reverse Logistics is the management of physical product flows returning from the customer to the supplier.

```
* Return flows are implemented to manage product repairs, 
* customer returns, 
* product modifications, 
* or recycling.
```

## Discover the different types


### Structure in the Supply Chai### n

```
* Return flows can be made from the customer to the direct supplier, 
* for example from the point of sale to the distribution center.

* The return can go back directly through several links in the Supply Chain, 
* for example from the point of sale to the factory.
```
### Sustainable Supply Chain

```
* Return flows are increasingly important due to European regulations 
* and growing customer demand for product recycling.
```

### Evolution and Strategic Importance

```
* Return flows must take into account the same 
* issues as the material flow from supplier to customer, 
* including the issue of first mile management 
* and transport optimization.
```
## Consider it from product design

### Regulatory evolution: towards a sustainable supply chain

```
* It has become more widespread with legislation, 
* requiring the recovery of certain products 
* and the development of sustainable Supply Chain. 

* Companies have had to adapt to implement a financially profitable one.
```

### Flow optimization: early integration

```
* To reduce total costs (main flow and Reverse Logistics), 
* you must take into account return flows 
* as early as possible in the chain. 

* This even has an impact on product design,
* meaning considering the need for reverse logistics 
* several years before its implementation.
```
### Eco-responsible design: sustainable products

- Product design must take into account the needs for:

```
* retrofit, for easier upgrading. 
* Retrofit is already taken into account during design in the aeronautical industry, for example;

* quality of components, 
* to limit the wear rate of products. 

* Planned obsolescence from the design stage 
* no longer gives a good image of the company, 
* and it is even repressed by the legislator;

* repairability or interchangeability 
* of parts with the highest probability of wear. 
```

## In summary

```
* Reverse logistics is the management 
* of return flows from the customer to the supplier.

* or by the customer (unsold items, after-sales service...).

* particularly with the adoption of European eco-recycling laws 
* and consumer demand to increase product repairability.
```
