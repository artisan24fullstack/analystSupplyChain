# Transport your finished products to consumption points

## Understand the constraints of product routing

```
* To transport products, you must fully integrate the constraints 
* associated with the chosen mode of transport,
* as well as the collateral effects induced on reactivity, 
* stock levels, and associated direct and indirect costs.
```
- PS : So, what are the different modes of transport available to you and how to choose

## Face the risks of transport

- The risks of transportation begin 

```
* in the definition of roles and responsibilities 
* between the supplier and the customer during the transport of goods.

* When do I take responsibility for the goods ? 
* Once delivered to me ? 
* On the supplier's shipping docks ? 
* Who handles customs ?
```

### International Transport Standards

- The respective obligations and responsibilities of the seller and buyer 
  
```
* are standardized for international trade, 
* these are called INCOTERMS.
```    
### Main Logistical Risks

- The two main issues in transportation are:
  
```
* to deliver on time in order to avoid paying late penalties;
* to deliver the product in the same physical condition as when it was loaded.
```

### Causes of Delay and Their Consequences

- Regarding delivery delays, this can come from a shipping delay and/or external disruptions during transport

### Protection of Goods

- The goods can also be damaged or lost during transport,


### Specific Security Solutions

- To limit the risks of damage during transport, specific means of transport are used,

### Protective Packaging

- Specific packaging has been invented to protect the product

## TMS tools

- In addition to specific packaging, 
- to optimize transport in terms of truck filling and delivery routes, carriers use a TMS tool. 
- For truck filling, the TMS will offer you the best Tetris of goods, which will limit empty spaces in the truck

## In summary

```
* The choice of transport mode depends first 
  on the constraints of the transported products (fresh products, dangerous goods...).

* Then, the best compromise between transport time and cost must be chosen.

* Transport must guarantee on-time delivery 
  and respect for the physical integrity of the goods.

* Now that the finished products are transported out of the factory
```
