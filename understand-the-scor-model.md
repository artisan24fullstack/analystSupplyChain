# Understand the SCOR Model of Supply Chain

## The SCOR model describes the processes:

```
Plan: 
Planning should enable the management of the following 5 processes

Order: 
How and under what payment terms will our customers order products? What service will they expect?

Source: 
Suppliers deliver the ingredients.

Transform: 
The product is manufactured and assembled into the finished product.

Fulfill: 
Delivery to customers, managing packaging, transportation, delivery, and invoicing.

Return: 
Managing the return of unsold products.
```  

## Recommends key indicators to monitor and manage these processes.

```
The main indicators are:

Customer lead time: 
What is my customer promise in terms of delivery time 

On-time delivery: 
Am I meeting my commitments to deliver to my customers ?

Flexibility: 
Do I adapt (in volume and product mix) to respond to customer orders ?

Quality: 
Does it reach the level expected and communicated to consumers ?

Costs: 
Am I aligning with the prices expected by my customers ?
```  

PS - These indicators align with the SCOR performance attributes of reliability, responsiveness, agility, quality, and cost.

### In summary

- The Supply Chain processes are described in the SCOR model: plan, source, make, deliver, return, and manage.
- The indicators for monitoring and controlling processes should be based on flows.
